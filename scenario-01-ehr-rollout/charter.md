# Project Charter — EHR System Rollout

> **Note:** This is a fictional case study created to demonstrate technical 
> project management skills. It is not based on a real employer, client, 
> or project.

## Project Name
Unified EHR Platform Implementation — Multi-Site Clinic Network

## Project Sponsor
VP of Clinical Operations

## Project Manager
Robert Younge

## Background
The clinic network currently operates on a legacy, on-premise EHR system 
that varies in configuration across its 6 sites. The system does not meet 
new state interoperability requirements, creates data silos between clinics, 
and increases administrative burden on clinical staff. The organization has 
selected a cloud-based EHR platform to replace it.

## Business Objectives
1. Achieve full compliance with state interoperability mandate by the 
   regulatory deadline
2. Standardize clinical and administrative workflows across all 6 sites
3. Reduce administrative time spent on charting and billing
4. Maintain uninterrupted patient care and zero unplanned downtime during 
   transition

## Project Scope

**In Scope**
- Data migration from legacy system to new EHR platform
- Configuration of new system for each clinic's specialty needs
- Staff training across all roles (clinicians, front desk, billing)
- Phased go-live across 6 sites
- Post-launch support and stabilization period

**Out of Scope**
- Replacement of hardware/devices (existing workstations will be reused)
- Integration with third-party research systems (planned for future phase)

## Success Criteria
- All 6 sites live on new platform within 9 months
- Zero HIPAA compliance findings in post-launch audit
- Staff adoption rate of 90%+ within 60 days of each site's go-live
- No more than 4 hours of unplanned downtime per site during cutover

## Key Stakeholders
- VP of Clinical Operations (Sponsor)
- Clinic Site Directors (6)
- IT Director
- Compliance Officer
- Clinical Staff (physicians, nurses, medical assistants)
- Front Desk / Billing Staff
- EHR Vendor Implementation Team

## High-Level Timeline
- **Month 1–2:** Planning, vendor kickoff, pilot site selection
- **Month 3–4:** Pilot site configuration, testing, training
- **Month 5:** Pilot site go-live and stabilization
- **Month 6–8:** Phased rollout to remaining 5 sites
- **Month 9:** Final site go-live, project closeout, post-implementation review

## Budget Authority
Project Manager authorized to approve expenditures up to $10,000 without 
additional sponsor sign-off; anything above requires VP approval.

## Assumptions
- Vendor resources are available per the agreed implementation schedule
- Clinic sites can allocate staff time for training without reducing patient 
  capacity below 80% during training weeks

## Constraints
- Hard regulatory deadline (state interoperability mandate)
- Limited IT staff bandwidth (shared across all 6 sites)
- Must maintain HIPAA compliance throughout migration
