# Training Plan — EHR System Rollout

> **Note:** This is a fictional case study created to demonstrate technical 
> project management skills. It is not based on a real employer, client, 
> or project.

## Purpose
Clinical staff adoption was identified as the highest-risk factor for this 
rollout. This training plan was designed to build competency and confidence 
before each site's go-live, not just deliver information.

## Training Approach
- **Role-based tracks** — physicians, nurses, medical assistants, front 
  desk, and billing staff each received training tailored to their specific 
  workflows, not a one-size-fits-all session
- **Train-the-trainer model** — one "super user" per site trained in depth 
  ahead of the rest of the staff, to provide on-the-floor peer support 
  during go-live
- **Hands-on practice environment** — staff practiced in a sandbox version 
  of the system using realistic (de-identified) sample patient data before 
  touching the live system

## Training Schedule (Per Site)

| Timing | Activity | Audience |
|--------|----------|----------|
| 4 weeks before go-live | Super user deep-dive training (2 days) | Designated site super users |
| 3 weeks before go-live | Role-based training sessions (half-day each) | All clinical & administrative staff |
| 2 weeks before go-live | Sandbox practice sessions | All staff |
| 1 week before go-live | Workflow dry-run / dress rehearsal | All staff |
| Go-live day + 5 business days | On-site hypercare support | Super users + vendor support on-site |

## Training Materials
- Role-specific quick reference guides (1-page cheat sheets)
- Recorded walkthrough videos for common workflows
- FAQ document updated weekly based on pilot site questions

## Success Metrics
- 100% of clinical staff complete role-based training before go-live
- 90%+ staff confidence rating (self-reported survey) before go-live
- Support ticket volume drops below baseline threshold within 2 weeks 
  post-launch

## Notes
- Feedback from the pilot site's training sessions was used to revise 
  materials before rolling out to the remaining 5 sites
- Front desk and billing staff initially had shorter training windows, but 
  this was extended after pilot feedback showed billing workflow confusion
