## Stakeholder List

| Stakeholder | Role/Interest | Influence | Interest | Engagement Strategy |
|-------------|---------------|:---:|:---:|----------------------|
| VP of Clinical Operations | Project Sponsor; accountable for outcomes to executive leadership | High | High | Weekly steering committee updates; direct escalation path |
| IT Director | Technical implementation, system configuration, infrastructure | High | High | Weekly working sessions; joint decision-making on technical risks |
| Compliance Officer | Ensures HIPAA and regulatory compliance throughout migration | High | High | Sign-off checkpoints at each migration milestone |
| Clinic Site Directors (6) | Operational leads at each site; manage local staff readiness | Medium | High | Biweekly calls; site-specific readiness checklists |
| Clinical Staff (physicians, nurses, MAs) | End users; workflow directly impacted | Low | High | Role-based training, super user support, feedback surveys |
| Front Desk / Billing Staff | End users; scheduling and billing workflow impacted | Low | High | Role-based training, dedicated billing reconciliation support |
| EHR Vendor Implementation Team | Delivers system configuration and technical support | Medium | Medium | Weekly sync calls; contractual SLAs |
| Patients | Indirect stakeholders; may notice temporary scheduling/workflow changes | Low | Low | Clinic signage, patient portal notices before go-live |

## Power-Interest Grid Summary
- **Manage Closely (High Influence / High Interest):** VP of Clinical Operations, IT Director, Compliance Officer
- **Keep Satisfied (High Influence / Lower Interest):** N/A for this project — all high-influence stakeholders also had high interest
- **Keep Informed (Lower Influence / High Interest):** Clinic Site Directors, Clinical Staff, Front Desk/Billing Staff
- **Monitor (Low Influence / Low Interest):** Patients

## Notes
- Clinical staff were classified as lower "influence" in formal decision-making 
  but their buy-in was treated as mission-critical to project success — 
  engagement effort was high despite lower formal influence
- Stakeholder register was reviewed at each project phase transition to 
  confirm engagement strategies were still effective
