# Post-Implementation Review — EHR System Rollout

> **Note:** This is a fictional case study created to demonstrate technical 
> project management skills. It is not based on a real employer, client, 
> or project.

## Purpose
Conducted 30 days after the final site's go-live to evaluate project 
performance against original success criteria and formally close the project.

## Success Criteria Review

| Success Criteria (from Charter) | Target | Actual | Status |
|----------------------------------|--------|--------|--------|
| All sites live within 9 months | 9 months | 9 months | ✅ Met |
| HIPAA compliance findings | 0 findings | 0 findings | ✅ Met |
| Staff adoption rate (within 60 days) | 90%+ | 95% | ✅ Exceeded |
| Unplanned downtime per site | ≤ 4 hours | Avg. 2.5 hours | ✅ Exceeded |
| Patient check-in time | Reduction expected | 18% reduction | ✅ Exceeded |

## Key Performance Metrics

- **Support ticket volume:** Returned to baseline within 2 weeks post-launch 
  at 5 of 6 sites; pilot site took 3 weeks due to initial billing workflow issues
- **Staff confidence survey (post-training):** Average 91% confidence rating 
  across all roles
- **Billing/claims processing:** No claim denial rate increase after the 
  2-week reconciliation window closed at each site
