# EHR System Rollout — Multi-Site Clinic Network

> **Note:** This is a fictional case study created to demonstrate technical 
> project management skills. It is not based on a real employer, client, 
> or project.

## Overview
As Project Manager, I led the replacement of a legacy, siloed electronic 
health record (EHR) system with a unified cloud-based EHR platform across 
a 6-clinic healthcare network serving 40,000+ active patients.

## Context
- **Organization:** Mid-sized outpatient clinic network (6 sites)
- **Staff affected:** ~150 clinical and administrative employees
- **Patients:** 40,000+ active patient records
- **Driver:** State regulatory mandate for interoperability compliance
- **Constraint:** No disruption to patient scheduling, billing, or care delivery

## Problem
- Each clinic operated on different workflows built around the legacy system
- Clinical staff were resistant to change, citing fear of workflow disruption
- HIPAA compliance had to be maintained throughout data migration
- Hard regulatory deadline with no flexibility

## Approach
- **Methodology:** Hybrid — Waterfall for regulatory/compliance milestones, 
  Agile sprints for system configuration and testing
- **Rollout strategy:** Phased by clinic (pilot site first, then staged 
  expansion) rather than a single network-wide go-live, to reduce risk

## Outcome
- Rolled out to all 6 sites within 9 months
- Reduced average patient check-in time by 18%
- Zero HIPAA compliance findings during post-launch audit
- Clinical staff adoption rate reached 95% within 60 days of each site's go-live

## Lessons Learned
- Early, hands-on training for clinical staff was the single biggest factor 
  in adoption success
- Piloting at one site before full rollout surfaced workflow issues that 
  would have been costly to fix at scale
- Weekly stakeholder syncs during the compliance review phase prevented 
  last-minute regulatory surprises
